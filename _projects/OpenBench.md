**推进陆面模式评估 | Advancing Land Surface Model Evaluation**

随着陆面模式(LSMs)不断发展，其中包含了从土壤水分动态到人类活动等复杂过程。然而，如何全面评估这些模型仍然是一个重大挑战。我们实验室开发的OpenBench是一个创新的评估系统，彻底改变了我们评估和改进LSMs的方式。

Land surface models (LSMs) are becoming increasingly sophisticated, incorporating complex processes from soil moisture dynamics to human activities. However, evaluating these models comprehensively has remained a significant challenge. Our lab has developed OpenBench, an innovative evaluation system that revolutionizes how we assess and improve LSMs.

**通用评估框架 | A Universal Evaluation Framework**

OpenBench在模型评估方法上代表着重大突破。与现有工具不同，它可以处理任何空间或时间分辨率的站点数据和网格数据。该系统包含超过40个评估指标和标准化评分指数，为模型性能提供了前所未有的深入见解。最重要的是，这是第一个全面评估人类活动对陆地表面过程影响的评估系统。

OpenBench represents a significant leap forward in model evaluation methodology. Unlike existing tools, it handles both station-based and gridded data at any spatial or temporal resolution. The system incorporates over 40 evaluation metrics and normalized scoring indices, providing unprecedented insight into model performance. Most importantly, it's the first evaluation system to comprehensively assess human impacts on land surface processes.

**创新特性 | Innovative Features**

多尺度评估 | Multi-scale Evaluation
OpenBench支持从站点到全球尺度的模型评估，使研究人员能够全面了解模型在不同空间范围内的表现。系统的灵活架构允许无缝集成新的数据源和评估方法。

OpenBench supports model evaluation from site to global scales, enabling researchers to gain comprehensive insights into model performance across different spatial domains. The system's flexible architecture allows seamless integration of new data sources and evaluation methods.

**人类影响评估 | Human Impact Assessment**

OpenBench独特地整合了评估人类活动（如灌溉、水库运行、土地利用变化）影响的功能。这使得研究人员能够量化人类活动对自然过程的改变程度，为改进模型中的人类活动参数化提供依据。

OpenBench uniquely incorporates capabilities to evaluate human activities such as irrigation, reservoir operations, and land use changes. This enables researchers to quantify the extent to which human activities modify natural processes, providing insights for improving the parameterization of human activities in models.

**未来展望 | Future Prospects**

随着气候变化和人类活动的影响日益显著，OpenBench将继续发展，整合更多评估指标和功能，为陆面模式的改进提供更全面的支持。我们期待与全球研究人员合作，共同推进陆面模式评估的科学发展。

As the impacts of climate change and human activities become increasingly prominent, OpenBench will continue to evolve, incorporating more evaluation metrics and functionalities to provide comprehensive support for land surface model improvement. We look forward to collaborating with researchers worldwide to advance the science of land surface model evaluation.
