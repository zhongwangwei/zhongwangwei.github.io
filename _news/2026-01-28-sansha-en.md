At the end of January 2026, I headed from Hainan to Yongxing Island in Sansha for work. The water there is unbelievably blue — every photo looks like a wallpaper.

Departed from Yelinwan (Coconut Grove Bay). Wide sandy beach, fishing nets out on the water — classic Hainan vibes.

## Yongxing Island

<div class="row mt-3">
    <div class="col-sm-12 mt-3 mt-md-0">
        {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/2026-01-28-sansha/sansha-yongxindao.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

Yongxing Island — 16°50.1'N, 112°19.8'E — the seat of Sansha City and the largest island in the Paracels. First stop: the landmark stone monument surrounded by palm trees.

The color is just unreal — turquoise, crystal clear, stretching all the way to the horizon. On a good day, the sea and sky just blend into one. Every shot turns out amazing.

## Shi Island (Stone Island)

<div class="row mt-3">
    <div class="col-sm-12 mt-3 mt-md-0">
        {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/2026-01-28-sansha/sansha-shidao.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}
    </div>
</div>
Shi Island has the highest elevation in the Paracels — all rocks and big waves. Standing between the reefs and watching the waves roll in one after another is pretty impressive.

## Picking Up Coral at Zhaoshu Island

<div class="row mt-3">
    <div class="col-sm-12 mt-3 mt-md-0">
        {% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/blog/2026-01-28-sansha/sansan-zhaoxudao.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}
    </div>
</div>
The water at Zhaoshu Island is incredibly clear. You can just pick up white coral pieces right from the shallows. Held one for a while — the marine life here is really something.

---

Great trip overall. Got the work done and saw some amazing scenery. Sansha is China's youngest and southernmost city — definitely worth a visit if you ever get the chance.
