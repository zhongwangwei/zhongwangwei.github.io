Returning to Tokyo after a long absence, it still feels quite familiar. But I have mixed feelings - I feel like I've aged, and I can't remember many things anymore. The University of Tokyo hasn't changed much, but the streets have changed significantly. I feel that Japanese traditions are gradually disappearing, and westernization is severe.
