## Food Recommendations

The Hunan restaurant "Shu Xiang Men Di" near HKU serves excellent food. Their signature dish of mashed chili with preserved eggs is particularly delicious and fragrant - definitely worth trying. Hong Kong is incredibly crowded, and HKU is even more so. I hope everyone appreciates their current office conditions, which are truly much better than those at HKU!
